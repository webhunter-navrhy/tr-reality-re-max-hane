BEACHFRONT HOTEL WITH A PRIVATE SANDY BEACH FOR SALE IN ŞİLE, ISTANBUL

Located on an approximately 3,000 m² plot in Şile, Istanbul, this operational hotel offers a private sandy beach area in addition to the land, a heated swimming pool and income-generating street-front retail units.

The property combines accommodation, beach club, restaurant, bar, wedding and event operations within a single investment.

KEY PROPERTY FEATURES

• Approximately 3,000 m² land area

• Additional private sandy beach area

• Direct beachfront location

• Operational 3-star hotel

• 48 rooms and 100-bed capacity

• Sea, pool and garden-view rooms

• Heated swimming pool

• Suitable for year-round operation

• Beach club and sunbathing areas

• Restaurant, bar, pool bar and beach bar

• Licensed for the sale and service of alcoholic beverages

• Wedding, reception and private event areas

• Stage suitable for concerts, DJs and live performances

• Corporate meeting and event facilities

• Large parking area

• Tenanted street-front retail units generating rental income

• Low-rise architecture integrated with nature

YEAR-ROUND INCOME POTENTIAL

During the summer, the property can operate through accommodation, private beach and beach club services. During the winter, its heated swimming pool, restaurant, weekend accommodation and event facilities support year-round operation.

Main potential income sources include:

• Room accommodation

• Daily beach club admissions

• Sunbed, cabana and private-area rentals

• Restaurant and bar operations

• Weddings, receptions and private events

• Concerts and live entertainment

• Corporate and branded events

• Rental income from street-front retail units

LOCATION

The hotel is situated within the Şile–Ağva tourism corridor, a popular coastal destination known for its beaches, natural bays and proximity to Istanbul.

Approximate driving times:

• Akçakese Bay: 8 minutes

• Ağlayan Kayalar: 18 minutes

• Ağva Beach and Ağva town centre: 19 minutes

• Şile town centre and Şile Lighthouse: 25 minutes

• Sabiha Gökçen International Airport: 68 minutes

Travel times may vary depending on traffic and road conditions.

WHY THIS PROPERTY?

Operational beachfront hotels in Istanbul with a private sandy beach, heated swimming pool and multiple income streams are extremely limited.

This investment stands out with:

• Access to Istanbul’s large customer market

• Private sandy beach

• Year-round operating potential

• Multiple income sources beyond accommodation

• Licensed restaurant and bar operations

• Wedding, concert and event capacity

• Rental income from street-front retail units
